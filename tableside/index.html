Tableside: Duo Tabletop Games

Twelve games for two people on one device, or one person against a computer. One upfront purchase for the complete collection. Offline play, no ads, no account.

Chess, Checkers, Reversi, Four in a Row, Dots and Boxes, Mancala, Memory Pairs, Tic-tac-toe, Air Hockey, Table Tennis, Flick and Bump, and Tug Duel.

Designed first for an opened iPhone Duo, with layouts for compatible iPhones and iPads.

Support

Choose Resume match in the cabinet to continue your saved game. Pause opens rules, restart and game-selection controls. Starting another match asks before replacing an unfinished saved match.

Email support@simplydose.app with the game, device model, system version and what happened. A screenshot is optional. Do not include payment details or private information you do not want to share. Email subject: Tableside support.
