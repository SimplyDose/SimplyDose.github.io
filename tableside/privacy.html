Tableside privacy policy.

Last updated October25,2026.

Tableside is published by SimplyDose LLC. The app runs its games and computer opponents offline and does not require an account.

Tableside saves one active match and your sound, haptics, player-mode and difficulty preferences in the app's local storage. Saved matches contain game positions, results, choices and settings needed to resume play. The app does not send gameplay to a developer-operated service and contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics services.

Your operating system may include app data in backups according to your backup settings. Starting another match replaces the saved match after the app's confirmation where applicable. Removing the app and its data removes local app storage. Copies in backups follow your backup provider's controls.

If you contact support, we process the contact information, message, attachments and follow-up records you provide to answer your request. Please do not send payment details or information that is unnecessary for support. We retain support communications as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate business or legal records.

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes under its own policies. The Tableside pages do not include a separate analytics SDK. Website requests and voluntary support emails are separate from the app's local gameplay storage. GitHub Pages data collection.

Apple handles App Store distribution and payments and may provide optional crash and performance reports under Apple's policies and your settings. Tableside does not use an additional purchase or crash-reporting SDK.

For privacy or support requests, contact support@simplydose.app and identify Tableside.
