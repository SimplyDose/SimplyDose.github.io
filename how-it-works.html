A focused record for the routine you log.

SimplyDose is an iPhone tracking app for people who want dose, weight, and side-effect records in one place.

01 · Set up

Create an account and enter the medication and routine details you choose to track.

02 · Start Premium

Tracking access after onboarding requires SimplyDose Premium. Apple confirms any eligible introductory offer and purchase terms.

03 · Record and review

Log entries, review the history you created, and prepare an appointment summary from those records.

Available on iPhone

SimplyDose supports iPhone on iOS 17 or later. It does not offer Android access. The app is a tracking tool, not medical advice or a medical device.

Account required. There is no anonymous tracking mode. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms before using SimplyDose.
