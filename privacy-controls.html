Privacy controls in the app.

SimplyDose includes account and data controls in Profile. This page is a product overview, not a replacement for the Privacy Policy or Terms.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Manage My Data → Delete Account. The app explains the deletion result before you confirm it.

Apple Health weight import

Apple Health weight access is optional and read-only. You can change Health access in iOS Settings.

Manage your subscription

Apple handles subscription management and cancellation in Apple Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

If you need help, contact support@simplydose.app.
