Before you get started.

Clear answers about tracking access and your first record.

Is SimplyDose free to use?

SimplyDose is free to download. An account and active SimplyDose Premium subscription are required for tracking features. Eligible new subscribers can try it free for 14 days. Apple confirms eligibility and localized pricing before purchase.

What do I do after downloading?

Complete setup and sign in, then review the subscription terms Apple shows you. With Premium active, start by recording a dose, your weight or a side effect.

Can I use my records for an appointment?

Yes. Visit Summary brings together records you logged so you can review them for an appointment. SimplyDose is a personal tracking tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Where do I manage my subscription?

Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple's Subscriptions settings. Subscriptions auto-renew until canceled. Your purchase terms and localized price are shown by Apple before you subscribe.
