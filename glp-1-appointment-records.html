Start with the records you already log.

Keep your entries together instead of searching through separate notes.

  • Dose dates, amounts and sites

    Look back at the medication and dose details you recorded. A logged entry is your record, not confirmation that a medication was taken.

  • Weight entries over time

    Review your dated weight entries and weight trends. These are personal tracking records, not a clinical assessment.

  • Side effects and how you felt

    Review what you recorded and when. SimplyDose does not determine the cause of a side effect.

Review a summary before your appointment.

Visit Summary brings together your logged records for the date range you choose.

Open Visit Summary in SimplyDose and choose the last 30, 60 or 90 days. Review the dates and entries in that range before using the summary for an appointment.

The summary reflects the information you entered. It does not replace your healthcare provider's advice or make medication decisions for you.

SimplyDose is a personal tracking tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider about your medication.
