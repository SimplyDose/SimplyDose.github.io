What should I track on GLP-1 shot day?

Record the date and time you took your dose, the medication and amount you took, and the injection site you used. If you notice a side effect later, note what you felt and when.

See the shot-day checklist

A checklist for shot day

  • Dose date and time

    In Log Dose, enter when you actually took the medication. Save the entry so you can check it later.

  • Medication and amount

    Record the medication and amount you took.

  • Injection site

    Record where you gave the injection. Your dose history gives you a place to check what you used last time.

  • Side effects and weight

    Log any side effects you notice on the day you experience them. If you choose to track weight, save dated entries so you can review the trend over time.

How can I use my records at my next appointment?

Look back at your dose dates, side-effect entries and weight history. Note anything you want to ask about. SimplyDose Visit Summary brings together the records you logged for the date range you choose.
